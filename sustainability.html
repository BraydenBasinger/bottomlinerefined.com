Sustainability

Select BLR projects yield environmental benefits, which we track and report.

Lower Water Usage

Reducing commercial laundry needs by reducing the quantity of uniforms, towels, and mats to be laundered

Lower Electric and Natural Gas Usage

Moving clients onto utility plans and rate classes that actually fit their usage, so facilities run efficiently without paying for more than they need

Reduced Greenhouse Gas and Hazardous Waste

Scheduling efficient pickups of garbage and shred to minimize fuel emissions

Reduced Landfill / Increased Recycling

Ensuring clients only purchase what they need while educating clients on the importance of recycling

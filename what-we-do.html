What We Do

A negotiated contract does not guarantee you receive the price, service, or terms you negotiated. BLR makes sure you do. We recover money you have already lost, prevent it from leaking again, and monitor your spend going forward.

Not only does BLR bring your costs down – we keep them down. Part of our negotiation with vendors involves locking in better pricing for long periods of time. You will continue to experience lower costs with minimal annual price increases. We are determined to save you money right away, but we are primarily concerned with long-term solutions that ensure better pricing and processes for years to come.

We guarantee you savings, or we don't get paid.

Our Services

Projects involve many contractors, change orders, and complex billing that can be hard to check line by line. Contracts often have grey areas that leave room for costly misinterpretation. It gets even more complicated when the scope of work was never clearly spelled out in the first place, because then there is no clear standard to audit against. BLR reviews your construction contracts and invoices to make sure you are only paying for work that was actually done, at the rates and terms you agreed to. We identify overcharges, unclear contract terms, and billing errors, then work with you to recover overcharges and tighten up future agreements. Clear contracts do more than prevent the next overcharge. They build trust, because both sides finally read the agreement the same way.

Freight billing is complex. Carriers send bills across many modes, like full truckload, less than truckload, and parcel. Rates, surcharges, and accessorial fees make it easy to overpay without noticing. BLR reviews carrier invoices for errors, duplicate charges, and rate mismatches. We also help manage the payment process itself, so carriers get paid correctly and on time. And every invoice we audit becomes data. We compile it into a clear picture of what you ship, with which carriers, at what cost. Then we help you use that picture to make better shipping decisions: which carriers deserve more of your freight, where a different mode would cost less, and which rates are due for renegotiation.

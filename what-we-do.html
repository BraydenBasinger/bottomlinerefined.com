What We Do

Not only does BLR bring your costs down – we keep them down. Part of our negotiation with vendors involves locking in better pricing for long periods of time. You will continue to experience lower costs with minimal annual price increases. We are determined to save you money right away, but we are primarily concerned with long-term solutions that ensure better pricing and processes for years to come.

We guarantee you savings, or we don't get paid.

Workers compensation premiums are a significant and ever-increasing company expense. The system that determines premiums is extremely complicated involving rating bureaus, insurance company underwriters, insurance agents, and more. In addition, the metrics used to establish rates are constantly changing and open to interpretation. Our audit program evaluates up to the last four years of a company's policies and premium calculations to determine if the correct amount of premiums have been charged. We look at all aspects of your premiums, including classifications, experience ratings, payroll audits, rates, and applicable discounts to ensure the lowest cost coverage. We make sure you get what you are paying for and are not paying for more than you want or need.

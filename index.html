Enterprise Spend Assurance

What We Do Who We Are

Recover. Prevent. Monitor.

Most companies spend a lot of time negotiating a good deal. Very few spend the same effort checking that they actually got it. That gap is where money quietly leaks out of a business. BLR closes that gap. We find money you have already lost, fix what caused it, and keep watching so it does not happen again.

Recover

We look backward first. We review your contracts and invoices to find overcharges, billing errors, and terms that were never followed.

Prevent

Finding a problem once is not enough. We fix the process that let it happen, so the same overcharge does not come back next year.

Monitor

We stay involved, checking invoices and vendor performance over time, and right-sizing your services as your needs change, so you are never paying for more than you need.

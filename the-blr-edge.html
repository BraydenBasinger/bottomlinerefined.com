The BLR Edge

Analyzing monthly invoices, with potentially hundreds of thousands of line items, demands advanced software.
BLR proprietary software goes beyond simple spreadsheets and is uniquely configured for each client to maximize savings.

Program capabilities include:

Profitability

• Actual vs. target savings analysis
• Monte Carlo probabilistic modeling
• Expense tracking by product
• Compliance with contract pricing

Record Keeping

• Line by line monthly invoice audit
• Employee headcount monitoring
• Reconciliation of line item detail reports to actual paid invoices
• Easy to read monthly client reports

Inventory Management

• Identifying and tracking monthly overcharges
• Monitoring unexpected inventory usage changes
• Inventory optimization
• Identifying invoice charges not specified in contract

The BLR Edge

A single vendor can send you hundreds of thousands of invoice line items a year. No team can check all of that by hand. So most companies do not. That is exactly what vendors count on.

BLR checks every line. Every invoice. Every month.

There is no off the shelf software that knows your contract. Our team builds custom software for each client and each spend category, designed around your contract terms, your pricing, and your operations. We use AI where it genuinely helps, like flagging unusual charges for review faster. Our people make every final call.

What our software does for you:

We learn your program

Before we build anything, we know your contract, your rates, your locations, and your vendor's habits. Usually because we have already audited the program by hand.

We build around it

The software is written for your program and nobody else's. It knows your pricing, your terms, your inventory, and the exact places your vendor tends to slip.

It never stops checking

Every invoice, every line, every month. Anything off gets flagged and reviewed by our team. You get a clear monthly report, your savings stay tracked, and your services stay sized to what you actually use.

Every audit teaches us something. Every engagement adds to what we know about how money leaks out of contracts. We are building that knowledge into spend intelligence, tools that spot the next problem before it costs you anything. The more we watch for you, the smarter the watching gets.

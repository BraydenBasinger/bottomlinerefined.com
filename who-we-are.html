Who We Are

Expense Savings, Risk Free.

Bottom Line Refined is a Southern California based consulting firm specializing in the analysis and reduction of select operating expenses. Our goal is to reduce and manage your most challenging expenses, leaving you with the freedom to focus on what really matters.

Throughout the full length of our contract, BLR will negotiate, implement and monitor the operations of all approved proposals in order to realize maximum savings while protecting the interests of your company. In order to provide a truly risk-free service to our clients, BLR shares in the savings only after they have been realized. If we are not able to save you money, there is no fee for our services. However, savings typically vary between 25-55%. As a CPA certified firm, we bring the same standard of accuracy to every audit that you would expect from a licensed accounting firm. With our wealth of experience, you can rely on us to develop innovative solutions that will refine the bottom line of your business.

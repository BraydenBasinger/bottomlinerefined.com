Who We Are

Spend Assurance, Risk Free.

Bottom Line Refined is a Southern California based consulting firm providing Enterprise Spend Assurance across a wide range of business expenses. Cost savings is one outcome of our work, but it is not the only one. We give you control over your third party spend: money recovered, contracts honored, vendors held accountable, and clear visibility into where every dollar goes. Our goal is to take your most challenging expenses off your desk, leaving you free to focus on what really matters.

For the full length of our contract, BLR negotiates, implements, and monitors every approved change while protecting your interests. Along the way you get more than a lower bill. You get vendors that follow the contract, invoices you can trust, processes that stop leaks before they start, and reporting that shows exactly where you stand. Our service is truly risk free: we only share in savings after they are real, and if we cannot save you money, there is no fee. Savings typically range from 25% to 55%. As a licensed CPA firm in California, we bring the accuracy you would expect from an accounting firm to every audit we perform. With decades of experience behind us, you can rely on BLR to protect your spend and refine the bottom line of your business.

The BLR Method

Our Process

Every BLR engagement follows the same idea. Recover what you have lost. Prevent it from happening again. Monitor your spend so the savings stick. And right-size your services along the way, so you only ever pay for what you actually need. Here is how that plays out step by step.

  • HOW IT STARTS

    Send us your recent invoices and contracts, and we will show you where money is leaking and how much we think we can recover. We only get paid from the savings we deliver. If we do not save you money, you owe us nothing.

  • RECOVER

    STEP ONE: FIND THE LEAKS

    We start by learning your operation. We review your current invoices, vendor contracts, and how your spend actually works day to day. This tells us where pricing does not match the contract, where service falls short, and where money is quietly slipping out.

  • RECOVER

    STEP TWO: RECOVER WHAT YOU ALREADY LOST

    We audit up to 48 months of past invoices to find errors and overbillings. When we find money you should not have paid, we go to the vendor on your behalf and get it back. Refunds come as a credit, a check, or a lower rate going forward.

  • PREVENT

    STEP THREE: FIX YOUR PRICING AND CONTRACTS

    We benchmark your rates against the market, negotiate the best possible pricing, and tighten the contract terms that let the overcharges happen. Then we look at the service itself. We match your service levels to what your operation actually uses, so you only pay for what you need.

  • MONITOR

    STEP FOUR: MONITOR AND PROTECT YOUR SAVINGS

    Savings erode when nobody is watching. We keep checking your invoices line by line every month for the full length of our agreement, using software we build around your contract. We track your savings, catch new errors early, handle vendor issues as they come up, and right-size your services as your needs change. We make sure the savings and service level are maintained.

  • MONITOR

    STEP FIVE: FIX YOUR PROCESS AND TRAIN YOUR TEAM

    If you would rather bring this in house, we fix the controls that let errors slip past and train your invoice approvers on exactly what to look for, so your own people catch problems before invoices get paid.
